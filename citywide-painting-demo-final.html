Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Do you offer free estimates?

Yes. Estimates are always free with no obligation, and in most cases we can get you a same-day quote. Call (310) 818-9100 and describe the property and scope.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes — Citywide Painting is licensed, bonded, and insured under CSLB License #1033985.

What areas do you serve?

We cover the South Bay and greater Los Angeles, including Torrance, Palos Verdes, Redondo Beach, Hermosa Beach, Manhattan Beach, El Segundo, San Pedro, Culver City, Santa Monica, Beverly Hills, and the surrounding communities.

Do you offer a warranty?

Every job is backed by our 4-year workmanship warranty. If something isn't right, we come back and make it right.

Do you handle HOA and commercial properties?

Yes. We regularly work with HOAs, property managers, and commercial clients, and we schedule around residents and business hours to keep disruption low.

What kind of paint do you use?

We use premium 100% acrylic coatings selected for color retention and chalk resistance — built for the Southern California climate.
